# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and
anywhere you represent the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome people of every background and
experience level, and keep criticism about the work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, and publishing anyone's private information (including their training or health data)
are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately, by email at
the address on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/dlamagna). Please don't report it in a
public issue. Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainer will acknowledge yours as soon as possible.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides on and applies the consequences described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
ladder: from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project, depending on the
seriousness and on whether the behaviour continues.
